Chronos AI is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0
(AGPL-3.0)**. That licence is free of charge and permits commercial use, but it
carries one significant obligation:

> If you modify Chronos AI and run it — including running it as a hosted or
> networked service — you must make your modified source code available to
> your users under the AGPL-3.0.

## When you need a commercial licence

You need a commercial licence if you want to:

- integrate Chronos AI into a **closed-source product**;
- offer a **hosted or SaaS service** built on Chronos AI without publishing
  your modifications;
- **sublicense** Chronos AI to your own customers;
- use it in an internal system your legal team will not allow to carry a
  copyleft obligation.

## When you do not

You do **not** need a commercial licence for:

- academic research, teaching or student work;
- heritage institutions, museums and non-profit archaeological units;
- evaluation, prototyping and internal experimentation;
- any use where you are content to publish your modifications under AGPL-3.0.

If you are a university, a museum, or a public heritage body, just ask — this
is exactly the audience the project was built for, and terms will be generous.

## How to request one

Open an issue titled `Commercial licence request`, or contact the copyright
holder using the details in the repository README. Please include:

1. Who you are (organisation, country);
2. What you want to build, and roughly at what scale;
3. Whether the result will be public, internal or commercial;
4. Whether you intend to redistribute.

Terms are negotiated individually. There is no published price list.

Versions released before 2026 under the MIT License remain available under the
MIT License and require no commercial licence. This applies to those releases
only.
